RESTORATION PLAN OF RECORD - RIO VERDE, BLOCK RV-7 (2025)

Objective: restore 500 hectares of degraded pasture inside polygon RV-7 to
native forest cover within twelve months of funding.

Planned method: active planting of 1,600 stems per hectare on 380 hectares;
assisted natural regeneration with fencing and fire protection on 120
hectares. Species list attached separately. Expected canopy closure above 30
percent by month twelve on at least 450 hectares, rising thereafter.

This document is a plan and a forecast. It records no achieved outcome.
