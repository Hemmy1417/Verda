PROJECT COMPLETION REPORT - RIO VERDE RESTORATION, BLOCK RV-7
Prepared by the project operator

Between 2025-10 and 2026-08 the team planted and maintained 480 hectares
across polygon RV-7 using a mix of active planting (native pioneer and
climax species, 1,600 stems per hectare) and assisted natural regeneration on
the northern slopes. Fire breaks were maintained throughout the 2026 dry
season; no fire events were recorded inside the polygon.

Survival monitoring continues on the operator's own plots and will be reported
quarterly. The operator considers the 500-hectare target substantially met.
