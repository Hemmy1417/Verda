FIELD ASSESSMENT NOTE
Site: RV-7 restoration block, Para, Brazil
Assessor: a second monitoring body engaged after the first ruling
Field dates: 2026-09-01 to 2026-09-02 (rapid transect survey)

FINDING
Using a stricter 1,400 live stems per hectare criterion, this survey
certified 300 hectares of polygon RV-7 as restored. Under the 1,100 stems per
hectare floor named in the agreement's terms, the same transects would
support materially more area; that figure was not computed.

This note was commissioned by a party to the agreement.
