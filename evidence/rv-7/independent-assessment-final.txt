INDEPENDENT FIELD ASSESSMENT - FINAL
Site: RV-7 restoration block, municipality of Paragominas, Para, Brazil
Assessor: Instituto Verde Campo (third-party monitoring body)
Field dates: 2026-08-20 to 2026-08-24
Plots surveyed: 40 permanent plots of 400 square metres, stratified across
the polygon

FINDING
Stem survival at or above 1,100 live stems per hectare was certified on 460
hectares of polygon RV-7. Forty hectares did not meet the survival floor,
mostly in the eastern strip where seasonal flooding delayed the 2025 planting
cohort.

BASIS
Plot counts extrapolated to the stratum they represent; survival measured on
tagged stems planted between 2025-10 and 2026-03. No planting outside RV-7
was inspected and none is certified here.

This assessment is issued to the funder and the operator jointly and is
published unaltered. Instituto Verde Campo has no financial interest in the
project's outcome.
