PROJECT COMPLETION REPORT - RIO VERDE RESTORATION, BLOCK RV-12
Prepared by the project operator

The team planted 470 hectares across polygon RV-12 between 2025-11 and
2026-07. Replanting of the flooded south-western pasture was completed in
2026-06 and canopy is expected to close there during the coming wet season.
The operator considers the block on track.
