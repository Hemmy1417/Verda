SATELLITE OBSERVATION SUMMARY
Polygon: RV-12 (Rio Verde restoration block), Para, Brazil
Product: Sentinel-2 L2A composite, 10 m resolution, cloud-masked
Acquisition window: 2026-08-01 to 2026-08-28

RESULT
Canopy cover above the 30 percent threshold was detected on 410 hectares of
the 500-hectare polygon RV-12. The remaining 90 hectares read below threshold,
concentrated in the south-western pasture that was replanted late after the
2026 flood.

METHOD
NDVI threshold classification against the 2025-09 baseline; hectare cells
with more than 30 percent covered area counted; cloud and shadow masked per
scene.

This summary states a measured result for polygon RV-12 only.
